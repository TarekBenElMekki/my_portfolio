ROS2 TELEOPERATED SURGICAL ROBOTIC INSTRUMENT
TECHNICAL PROJECT BRIEF

PROJECT TYPE
Professional Master's thesis project.

OBJECTIVE
Design and prototype a teleoperated surgical instrument combining mechanical
articulation, ROS2 control, camera feedback, force sensing and safety logic.

SOFTWARE ARCHITECTURE
- ROS2 Humble
- MoveIt and RViz on the computer
- Raspberry Pi hardware-control layer
- FollowJointTrajectory-compatible execution
- Camera and force-sensor topics

HARDWARE
- Raspberry Pi 4B
- Three servo motors
- Cable-and-pulley mechanical transmission
- FSR force sensor
- MCP3202 analog-to-digital converter
- OV5647 camera

SAFETY
The system warns before excessive force and blocks additional jaw closing when
the configured safety threshold is reached. Opening remains available.

MAIN CONTRIBUTIONS
Mechanical design, ROS2 integration, motion control, embedded programming,
sensor acquisition, camera integration and system testing.
